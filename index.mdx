Controlador de Fechas is a local automation tool for QA-testing time-gated rewards in Android games. It drives your phone through a precise sequence — rolling the device clock back, opening the game, switching to the browser, restoring the real date, and executing a configurable tap sequence to claim the reward — all without you touching the phone. The entire control surface lives in a browser tab served by a pure-PowerShell HTTP server that ships zero external dependencies.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your Android device">
    Plug your phone in via USB, enable USB debugging, and authorize the ADB connection when prompted on the device screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the server">
    Double-click `iniciar.bat`. A PowerShell window opens and your browser navigates automatically to `http://127.0.0.1:8790`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure a profile">
    Enter your game's package name, set how many days to roll back, and build the tap sequence that claims the reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the test">
    Click **Iniciar prueba**. The panel shows live progress, iteration count, and a streaming log. Click **Detener** at any time — the tool always restores the real device clock before it stops.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The server only listens on `127.0.0.1` and requires no internet connection. All HTML, CSS, and JavaScript are served from the local `public/` directory.
</Note>
